Financial Transactions

A financial transaction is an economic event that can be measured in monetary terms and affects the financial position of a business.

Common financial transactions include:

1. Capital Investment
When an owner invests cash into the business:
Debit: Cash
Credit: Owner's Capital

2. Payment of Rent
When rent is paid:
Debit: Rent Expense
Credit: Cash or Bank

3. Payment of Salary
When salary is paid:
Debit: Salary Expense
Credit: Cash or Bank

4. Purchase of Equipment for Cash
When equipment is purchased using cash:
Debit: Equipment
Credit: Cash

5. Receipt from Customer
When money is received from a customer:
Debit: Cash or Bank
Credit: Accounts Receivable

6. Payment to Supplier
When an amount owed to a supplier is paid:
Debit: Accounts Payable
Credit: Cash or Bank

Before recording a transaction, identify:
- What happened?
- Which accounts are affected?
- Which account should be debited?
- Which account should be credited?
- What is the transaction amount?

The accounting entry should maintain the equality between total debits and total credits.
