Journal Entries

A journal entry is a record of a financial transaction in an accounting system.

A basic journal entry contains at least one debit account and one credit account.
The total debit amount must equal the total credit amount.

Debit represents the left side of a journal entry.
Credit represents the right side of a journal entry.

Common examples:

1. Cash Expense
When an expense is paid using cash:
Debit: Expense Account
Credit: Cash

Example:
A company pays ₹5,000 for office supplies in cash.
Debit: Office Supplies Expense ₹5,000
Credit: Cash ₹5,000

2. Credit Purchase
When goods are purchased on credit:
Debit: Purchases or Inventory
Credit: Accounts Payable

Example:
Goods worth ₹20,000 are purchased on credit.
Debit: Inventory ₹20,000
Credit: Accounts Payable ₹20,000

3. Cash Sale
When goods are sold for cash:
Debit: Cash
Credit: Sales Revenue

Example:
Goods are sold for ₹15,000 in cash.
Debit: Cash ₹15,000
Credit: Sales Revenue ₹15,000

4. Credit Sale
When goods are sold on credit:
Debit: Accounts Receivable
Credit: Sales Revenue

Every journal entry should be supported by the relevant transaction information.
